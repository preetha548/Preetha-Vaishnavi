Lotus Biscoff Cheesecake

Enjoy the perfect combination of creamy cheesecake, caramelized Biscoff biscuits and smooth Biscoff spread.

⏱️

30 Minutes

Preparation Time

❄️

6 Hours

Chilling Time

🍰

8 Servings

Serves

🛒 Ingredients

250 g Lotus Biscoff biscuits
100 g Melted butter
500 g Cream cheese
200 ml Whipping cream
200 g Lotus Biscoff spread
80 g Powdered sugar
1 tsp Vanilla essence
Extra Biscoff biscuits for decoration

👩‍🍳 How to Make

1

Prepare the Biscuit Base

Crush the Lotus Biscoff biscuits into fine crumbs. Mix them with melted butter until well combined.

2

Set the Base

Press the biscuit mixture firmly into the bottom of a cheesecake tin. Refrigerate for 20–30 minutes.

3

Make the Filling

Beat the cream cheese and powdered sugar until smooth. Add vanilla essence and Biscoff spread.

4

Add Whipping Cream

Whip the cream until fluffy and gently fold it into the cheesecake mixture.

5

Assemble the Cheesecake

Pour the cheesecake filling over the chilled Biscoff base and smooth the top.

6

Chill

Refrigerate for at least 6 hours. For the best results, chill overnight.

7

Decorate and Serve

Add Biscoff spread and decorate with crushed or whole Biscoff biscuits before serving.
