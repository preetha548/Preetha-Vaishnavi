💚 Pistachio Delight

Experience the delicious taste of homemade pistachio stuffed cookies. These golden, buttery treats have a soft outer layer and a rich nutty filling. Perfect for celebrations and special occasions!

👩‍🍳 Preparation Method

01Prepare Filling

Mix chopped pistachios, powdered sugar, cardamom and melted butter. Make small filling balls and keep aside.

02Prepare Dough

Mix semolina, flour, sugar, baking powder and salt. Add butter and milk gradually. Knead into soft dough and rest for 20 minutes.

03Shape Cookies

Divide dough into equal portions. Flatten each portion and place the pistachio filling in the centre. Seal carefully.

04Bake Cookies

Preheat the oven to 180°C. Place cookies on a baking tray. Bake for 15–20 minutes until lightly golden.

05Decorate & Serve

Cool cookies completely. Decorate with powdered sugar and chopped pistachios. Serve with tea or coffee.

💚 Special Cooking Tips

  • ✔ Use fresh pistachios.
  • ✔ Do not make the dough too sticky.
  • ✔ Seal the filling properly.
  • ✔ Avoid overbaking.
  • ✔ Cool completely before serving.
  • ✔ Store cookies in an airtight container.
