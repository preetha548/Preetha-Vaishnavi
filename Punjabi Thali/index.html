1. Chana Masala

Spicy and delicious chickpea curry.

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup boiled chickpeas
  • 2 chopped onions
  • 2 chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Oil, salt and coriander leaves

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add onions and fry until golden brown.
  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes.
  4. Add all the spice powders and salt.
  5. Add boiled chickpeas and mix well.
  6. Add water and cook for 10–15 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander leaves.

2. Aloo Gobi

Traditional potato and cauliflower curry.

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cauliflower
  • 2 chopped potatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt and oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Heat oil and add cumin seeds.
  2. Add chopped onions and tomatoes.
  3. Add turmeric, chilli powder and salt.
  4. Add potatoes and cauliflower.
  5. Mix all ingredients thoroughly.
  6. Cover and cook for 15–20 minutes.
  7. Garnish with coriander leaves.

3. Dal Tadka

Healthy and flavourful yellow lentil curry.

Cooking Time: 25 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tomato
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • Salt and ghee

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and pressure-cook the dal with turmeric.
  2. Mash the cooked dal.
  3. Heat ghee in a separate pan.
  4. Add cumin seeds, garlic and red chilli.
  5. Add onions and tomatoes.
  6. Pour the tempering into the dal.
  7. Add salt and simmer for 5 minutes.

4. Chapati

Soft and healthy Indian wheat flatbread.

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour
  • Water as required
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 1 tsp oil

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix wheat flour, salt and oil.
  2. Add water gradually.
  3. Knead into a soft dough.
  4. Rest for 15 minutes.
  5. Divide the dough into small balls.
  6. Roll into round shapes.
  7. Cook on a hot tawa on both sides.

5. Poori

Golden and crispy deep-fried Indian bread.

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups wheat flour
  • Salt as required
  • Water as required
  • Oil for deep frying

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix wheat flour and salt.
  2. Add water and prepare firm dough.
  3. Rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Make small dough balls.
  5. Roll into small circles.
  6. Heat oil in a deep pan.
  7. Deep-fry until golden and puffed.

6. Steamed Rice

Simple, fluffy and delicious white rice.

Cooking Time: 20 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 2 cups water (adjust to rice variety)
  • A pinch of salt (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly.
  2. Add rice and water to a pressure cooker.
  3. Cook until the rice is soft.
  4. Allow pressure to release naturally.
  5. Fluff the rice gently.
  6. Serve hot.

7. Fresh Vegetable Salad

Healthy and refreshing vegetable salad.

Preparation Time: 10 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 onion
  • ½ lemon
  • Salt and pepper

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash all vegetables.
  2. Cut cucumber, tomato and onion.
  3. Put them into a bowl.
  4. Add lemon juice.
  5. Add salt and pepper.
  6. Mix and serve fresh.

8. Masala Buttermilk

Cool and refreshing traditional Indian drink.

Preparation Time: 5 Minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup curd
  • 2 cups chilled water
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • Coriander leaves
  • A pinch of salt
  • Grated ginger (optional)

Preparation Steps

  1. Add curd and chilled water to a blender.
  2. Blend until smooth.
  3. Add cumin powder and salt.
  4. Add grated ginger if desired.
  5. Mix well.
  6. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  7. Serve chilled.
